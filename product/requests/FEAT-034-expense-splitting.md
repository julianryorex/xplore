## Problem

Group trips involve shared costs; users already use Splitwise in parallel. Adjacent job-to-be-done increases stickiness.

## Proposed solution

Trip ledger built into the trip: snap a receipt and Xplore splits it by **who was at that stop**, with equal or custom splits as overrides and running balances. At the end, everyone settles up in one payment each.

**Trip kitty:** members pool money up front for shared costs. Top-ups by bank transfer are free; card top-ups carry a small fee. Included with Trip Pass and Plus (FEAT-020).
